What's inside

Practical tools. One system.

Every tool is built on applied neuroscience and designed to be used daily — not occasionally.

Daily Check-in
Clarity, confidence, motivation — scored every day. The baseline your coach reads before every session.
Guided Breathing
Box breathing and other protocols to shift your nervous system on demand. Pre-competition, post-training, or any time.
Visualisation Audio
Guided audio sessions for mental rehearsal. Mentally compete before you physically compete.
Goal Setting Framework
3-month, 12-month and 2-year goals — with outcome, performance target, confidence level and brain intention for each.
Coaching Journal
Daily reflection, motivation check, identity script. The written training your coach reviews alongside your scores.
Focus Training
Stroop test and Concentration Grid — timed, scored, tracked. Build and measure attention under cognitive load.
